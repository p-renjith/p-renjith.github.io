## 🔬 About Me & Research Interests
I am an Assistant Professor at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, National Institute of Technology Calicut. My primary research lies in theoretical computer science, specializing in structural and algorithmic graph theory.

**Core Research Interests:**
- **Graph Theory & Graph Algorithms**
- **Spanning Trees:** Constrained variants, structural properties, optimization, and generalizations.
- **Theoretical Computer Science**
